# The functional review of 6 October 2026

An outside review (eleven reviewers, 42 findings and 15 smaller concerns) was checked against the code, one finding at a time. This page
records what each one came to, so that nobody has to redo the checking.

How a finding was checked, and what the word means below:

- **Reproduced**: the review's example was run against the code as it was, and it went wrong the way the review said. The same example is
  now a test (or, where the test needs a browser, a script run in Chrome) that fails on the old code and passes on the new.
- **Read**: the claim follows from the source, but it was not run against the old code (the review itself marked several as "source
  analysis"). The fix has a test of its own.
- **Measured**: a claim about an exchange was checked on the live feed.

Nothing here was taken on trust, and two of the findings turned out not to be defects.

## The smaller concerns

- **Fixed**: the WASM build deleted the working assets before the new ones existed; the venue dialog could start a polling timer after it
  was closed; the AudioContext suspending did not update the sound status; expired prints stayed in memory when they left the store;
  footprint rows of a decimal step put a price at a boundary into the row below (3000.0125 / 0.0125 is 240000.99999999997); Bitfinex's
  `TRXUSD` and `TONUSD` lost their first letter; a recorder that could not write dropped its minutes instead of trying again; print
  limits are validated.
- **Not defects**: Bitget's `pseq` and the size of its sequence numbers (measured on the live `books` channel: 191 updates, each with a
  `pseq`, all chained, numbers around 10^12); a padded flow extent passing a freshness check (the padding is zeros, which cannot make a
  burst); replaying executions after a restart (no source replays them: `state.trades` starts empty).
- **Left as they are, on purpose**: the CVD hover time is the column's centre while its value is the running total at its end (half a
  column apart at most); the price lookup expires after two hours of silence while the plotted strip carries the last traded price
  (a point reading must not quote a stale price, a flat line is how "no trade since" is drawn); BitMEX's quanto `ETHUSD` is not valued
  (its dollar size needs a second price, and the book is withheld rather than mis-valued; it is not a default symbol).
