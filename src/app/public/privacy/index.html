Privacy

LiquidityMapperFast · last changed 17 November 2026

In short

The page has no account, sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising. Your settings, drawings and scripts are kept in your own browser. With Plus, a payment is handled by Paddle, and this site's server keeps the least it needs to know that a subscription is paid. The checkout on the Plus page is Paddle's and may set the cookies Paddle needs to take a payment.

What stays in your browser

Settings, layouts, drawings, scripts and recorded market data are stored in your browser's own storage on your device. They are not sent to this site. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes them.

What your browser asks for

  • The page's files, from Cloudflare, which hosts the site.
  • Market data, straight from the exchanges' public services. Each exchange sees a request from your address, as any website you visit does.
  • Recorded history and liquidation levels, from this site's data servers, through Cloudflare.
  • With Plus: fresh passes, from this site's Plus server (plus.hiverust.dev), about once a day (more often while it cannot be reached).

These services see what any web server sees: your IP address, the time, the file asked for and your browser's description. Cloudflare keeps such logs for a short time for security and to count traffic. This site does not combine them into profiles and does not track you across visits.

Plus

  • Payment. The checkout is Paddle's. Paddle is the seller of record and handles your name, email, address, tax details and payment method under its own privacy policy (paddle.com/legal/privacy). This site never sees your card or bank details.
  • What this site's server keeps. The subscription's reference at Paddle, its state (paid or not, and until when), and a scrambled form of the keys given to your devices. Paddle also lets the publisher see your email and invoices, which are used only to answer you and to find your subscription when you ask.
  • The key and the pass. Your browser keeps a key. About once a day it sends the key to this site's Plus server and gets a short-lived pass back, and it sends that pass with each request for a Plus file, so the server can tell the request is from a subscriber. The pass names the subscription by Paddle's reference for it; neither the key nor the pass holds your name or email.
  • Bringing Plus to another device. If you type your email and invoice number on the Plus page, they are sent to this site's server, compared with Paddle's record, and not stored.

How long

Subscription records are kept while the subscription runs and for as long as tax and accounting rules require afterwards. Paddle keeps its own records under its policy.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted, by writing to support@liquiditymapper.com. For payment data, write to Paddle. Depending on where you live, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Who is responsible
